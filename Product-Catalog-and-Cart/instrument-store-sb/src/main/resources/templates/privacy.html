Privacy Policy
Last updated July 2026

What we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order/payment history so we can process and fulfil your purchases.

How we protect it

Passwords are never stored in plain text - they're hashed before being saved. Payment details are handled through our checkout process and are not stored in plain form. Access to customer data in our admin tools is limited to authorized store staff.

How we use it

We use your information to process orders, coordinate delivery, send order/delivery/account notifications, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Emails

We email you for account verification, password resets, order confirmations, and delivery updates. These are transactional emails tied to your account activity, not marketing.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile information at any time from My Profile, and you can permanently delete your account from the same page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via the Contact page.
